✨ Core Features

Everything You Need to Manage Projects

Powerful board, list, and card management inspired by Trello.

Board Management

  • Create unlimited boards
  • Public & private visibility
  • Custom backgrounds & colors

List Management

  • Create & reorder lists
  • Edit titles inline
  • Archive completed lists
  • Drag-and-drop ordering

Card Management

  • Labels, start & due dates
  • Attachments & covers
  • Comments & activity history
  • Member assignments & archiving
🔔 Notification System

Stay Informed, Never Miss a Beat

Configure notifications for all major board, list, and card actions. Processed asynchronously for a seamless experience.

⚡
Async Processing

Instant notifications are processed asynchronously using Celery workers. Daily and weekly notification scheduled using Celery Beat.

  • 📧 Instant email notifications
  • 📊 Daily email notifications
  • 📅 Weekly email notifications
  • 🔕 Option to disable notifications
